Business Data Cleaning & Preparation

Portfolio Artifact 2 | AIML-500 Professional Portfolio

Project Overview

This project demonstrates the importance of preparing raw business data before performing analysis. Using a real-world concessions dataset, I identified data quality issues, corrected inconsistencies, removed duplicate records, validated the information, and documented the complete cleaning process. The objective was to improve the reliability of the dataset so it could support accurate business insights and decision-making.

Business Challenge

Organizations rely on high-quality data to make informed decisions. However, raw datasets often contain duplicate records, inconsistent formats, missing values, and data entry errors. If these issues are not addressed before analysis, they can lead to misleading reports and inaccurate business conclusions.

Data Quality Issues Identified

  • Duplicate records
  • Missing or incomplete values
  • Inconsistent date formatting
  • Incorrect text entries
  • General data validation issues

Data Cleaning Process

Identify Problems

Reviewed the dataset to identify duplicate records, formatting inconsistencies, and missing information that could affect analysis.

Clean the Data

Removed duplicate records, standardized formats, corrected inconsistent entries, and verified data accuracy throughout the worksheet.

Validate Results

Performed validation checks to ensure the cleaned dataset was complete, consistent, and ready for business analysis.

Document Changes

Created documentation describing the issues identified and the steps taken to improve the dataset, ensuring the cleaning process was transparent and repeatable.
