Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs)

Artificial Neural Networks are inspired by the structure of the human brain. They consist of interconnected neurons that learn patterns from data through training. ANNs are commonly used for prediction, classification, and pattern recognition tasks.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

Convolutional Neural Networks specialize in image processing by automatically detecting visual features such as edges, shapes, and objects. CNNs are widely used in facial recognition, medical imaging, autonomous vehicles, and computer vision applications.

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)

Recurrent Neural Networks process sequential information by retaining knowledge from previous inputs. They are commonly applied in speech recognition, language translation, text prediction, and time-series forecasting.

Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)

Generative Adversarial Networks consist of two neural networks working together to generate realistic synthetic data. GANs are widely used for image generation, creative AI applications, and synthetic data creation while also raising important ethical concerns regarding deepfakes.

Activation Functions

Activation functions determine whether neurons should activate and pass information through the network. They enable neural networks to learn complex, non-linear relationships and significantly improve model performance.
