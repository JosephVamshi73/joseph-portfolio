Project Overview

This project examines recent commercial applications of Artificial Intelligence across three industries: aerospace, healthcare, and financial services. The goal was to understand how organizations are using AI to improve business processes, support decision-making, increase efficiency, and create new opportunities.

The project also helped me look at AI from a business perspective rather than only focusing on the technical side. By examining real-world applications, I developed a better understanding of how AI technologies can be connected to specific organizational needs.

Industries Examined

Aerospace

AI is being applied to autonomous aircraft and other aerospace systems to support decision-making, improve operational capabilities, and reduce the need for continuous human intervention.

Healthcare

AI is being incorporated into medical technologies to support healthcare professionals, improve procedures, and provide new ways to use technology in patient care.

Financial Services

Financial organizations are using AI assistants and intelligent tools to help customers and financial institutions turn information into useful insights and actions.

Commercial AI Applications

Aerospace: Honeywell Aerospace and Shield AI

Honeywell Aerospace and Shield AI announced a collaboration focused on advancing autonomous aircraft development. This type of AI application demonstrates how intelligent systems can support autonomous operations and decision-making in aerospace environments.

The business value includes opportunities to improve aircraft capabilities, support complex missions, and reduce reliance on manual processes. As autonomous systems continue to develop, AI could play an increasingly important role in aerospace operations.

Healthcare: Johnson & Johnson MONARCH™ Platform

Johnson & Johnson introduced the MONARCH™ Platform to the Middle East as an example of technology being used in healthcare procedures. AI-enabled and advanced robotic technologies can assist healthcare professionals by providing greater precision and new capabilities during medical procedures.

The potential business and healthcare impact includes improved procedural capabilities, better use of technology by medical professionals, and continued development of digitally enabled healthcare services.

Financial Services: Visa AI Financial Assistant

Visa introduced an AI Financial Assistant designed to help banks guide customers from financial insights toward actions. This demonstrates how AI can be used to make financial information more useful and accessible to customers.

The application can help financial institutions improve customer engagement and provide more personalized support. AI assistants may also help organizations process information more efficiently and respond to changing customer needs.

Business Impact

Across these three industries, AI is moving beyond experimentation and becoming part of real business processes. The examples show that AI can support automation, decision-making, customer engagement, operational efficiency, and specialized professional tasks.

Key Insight:

The value of commercial AI is not only determined by how advanced the technology is. Its value also depends on whether it solves a meaningful business problem and provides measurable benefits to organizations and their customers.

Future Potential

The continued development of AI could lead to greater automation and more intelligent decision-support systems across many industries. Aerospace organizations may expand autonomous capabilities, healthcare organizations may continue integrating AI into medical technologies, and financial institutions may use AI to provide more personalized services.

At the same time, organizations will need to consider accuracy, security, privacy, responsible use, and human oversight when adopting AI. Successful AI implementation requires a balance between technological capabilities and responsible business practices.
